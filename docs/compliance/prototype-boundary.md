# Compliance and safety checklist — prototype

- The first test cohort is adults 18+; the current gate is self-declared and is not verified age assurance.
- No free-text input, image uploads, public UGC, open chat, AI companion or user-to-user messaging.
- No sensitive personal data is requested or stored on the server.
- Progress and choices are stored in browser local storage and can be cleared from the Story Shelf.
- No payment, subscription, ads, global ranking or behavioural profiling.
- AI-generated content is not generated at runtime. Any future AI-assisted creative assets require source, model, licence and human-review records.
- KURU name and design rights, original source files, prompts, model terms, trademark availability and any third-party assets must be audited before monetization.
- Mainland China public launch requires counsel review of applicable internet content, AI-generated content marking, personal information, algorithm, filing and app/service obligations.
- Overseas launch requires a target-market-specific review of privacy, consumer protection, age assurance, payments, taxes, copyright and content moderation.
- This checklist is not legal advice or proof of compliance.
