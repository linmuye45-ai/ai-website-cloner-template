# Signal Atlas — frozen supporting asset

This file supersedes the earlier draft that incorrectly recommended Signal Atlas as the primary commercial product.

The approved 2026-11-27 strategy makes NEXORA (internal project code) the sole active commercial validation track: an original-IP interactive micro-narrative network. Signal Atlas is a static-first research prototype with no verified live data pipeline, paid data rights, accounts, database or automated ingestion. It is frozen as a supporting research asset and must not receive parallel product investment.

Do not merge Signal Atlas into NEXORA's user-facing product. If it is ever reused, it may support internal competitor research or content-market research only, and only when that work directly advances the approved NEXORA strategy.

Primary strategy: `docs/strategy/nexora-interactive-micro-narrative.md`.

No Signal Atlas revenue forecast or moat claim is approved.
