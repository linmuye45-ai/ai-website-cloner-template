# 2026–2046 long-range thesis

## Strategic destination
Build a global commerce intelligence and execution network: a neutral product-truth layer connecting manufacturers, brands, creators, marketplaces, service providers and future AI purchasing agents.

## Why this sequence
1. Start with cross-channel product operations: low capital intensity, measurable merchant pain, no inventory ownership.
2. Accumulate verified product, channel, content-version and performance data with merchant consent.
3. Expand into creator distribution, procurement workflows and transaction orchestration only when network density exists.
4. Add logistics, manufacturing automation and physical-world execution through partners before owning capital-intensive assets.

## Defensible asset progression
- Product graph: canonical SKU, variant, attribute, material and compliance facts.
- Workflow graph: approvals, edits, channel rules and operator actions.
- Outcome graph: content version linked to impressions, conversion, orders, returns and quality.
- Supply graph: supplier capability, MOQ, lead time, quality and fulfilment evidence.
- Agent execution layer: permissioned tools and workflows across connected systems.

## Strategic prohibitions
- Do not launch a consumer marketplace before acquiring supply and repeat demand.
- Do not build a generic LLM wrapper or single-channel listing generator.
- Do not own inventory, payment custody or logistics fleets in the first phase.
- Do not scrape or automate accounts contrary to platform terms.
- Do not expand into unrelated verticals before the commerce network has durable economics.

## Milestones
- 2026–2028: prove one vertical, paid pilots and repeat workflow use.
- 2028–2031: multi-category, multi-market merchant operating layer.
- 2031–2036: permissioned product and supply graph, creator and transaction network.
- 2036–2046: broader intelligent procurement and physical supply-chain execution.

Company scale, profitability and valuation remain uncertain outcomes. Capital allocation must follow validated retention, contribution margin and network effects.
