# 90-day validation plan

## Days 1–14: problem discovery
- Interview 30 qualified merchants and 5 cross-border service providers.
- Observe the real workflow from source SKU to live listings on two channels.
- Collect anonymized spreadsheets, attribute maps, approval steps and listing rejection reasons.
- Quantify labour time, revision cycles, error rate and localization cost.
- Pass gate: at least 12/30 merchants independently report the same cross-channel product-truth problem; at least 5 agree to a paid pilot.

## Days 15–30: concierge MVP
- Onboard 5 design partners manually.
- Import one catalog through Shopify-compatible CSV.
- Produce a fact-safe market pack for one market and one channel.
- Require human review before external publication.
- Measure time saved, correction rate and output acceptance.

## Days 31–60: paid pilots
- Target 10 paid pilots; free usage is not willingness to pay.
- Add version history, approval state, export and audit trail.
- Add one read-only integration only if API and app-review requirements are clear.
- Measure weekly active workspaces, repeat workflows, gross margin per run and support hours.

## Days 61–90: retention and expansion
- Target >=70% of pilot workspaces completing a second workflow within 14 days.
- Target >=50% reduction in measured product-content preparation time.
- Target >=90% of generated product facts accepted without factual correction after merchant review.
- Target positive contribution margin before paid acquisition.
- Secure at least 5 customer references or quantified case studies.

## Kill / pivot criteria
- Fewer than 5 paid pilots after 30 qualified interviews: revisit ICP or pain.
- Repeat usage below 40% after 30 days: this is a one-off tool, not an operating layer.
- More than 20% of outputs require material factual correction: tighten ingestion and human review.
- Channel integrations require disproportionate custom work: stay export-first.
- CAC payback cannot plausibly fall below 12 months: do not scale paid acquisition.

## Pricing hypotheses — test, do not assume
- Starter: RMB 299–599/month.
- Growth: RMB 999–2,499/month.
- Business: RMB 4,999+/month.
- AI usage: included allowance plus transparent overage.
Do not publish prices until at least 10 paid conversations establish willingness to pay.
