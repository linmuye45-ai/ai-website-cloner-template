# Unit economics and pricing model

All figures are hypotheses for customer discovery, not validated prices or forecasts.

## Initial buyer
Cross-border apparel, footwear and accessories brands with 10–500 active SKUs, at least two sales channels and recurring content/localization work.

## Value metric
Charge against operational value: managed SKUs, active markets/channels, team seats and verified AI workflow usage. Avoid unlimited-generation plans until model cost and abuse controls are measured.

## Price tests
| Tier | Hypothesis | Intended customer |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | RMB 299–599/month | One operator, limited SKU count |
| Growth | RMB 999–2,499/month | Small team, multiple markets/channels |
| Business | RMB 4,999+/month | Larger catalog, approval controls and audit trail |

## Contribution margin
Track per workspace: subscription revenue + usage revenue − model/API cost − storage/egress − payment fees − support labour − partner/API costs. Measure gross margin by workflow and customer cohort before paid acquisition.

## Guardrails
- Do not subsidize unlimited image/video generation.
- Show credits and estimated cost before expensive jobs.
- Keep external API usage within provider terms and platform rate limits.
- Do not count gross merchandise value as NEXUS revenue.
- Do not scale CAC until repeat use and retention are demonstrated.

At RMB 1,000 monthly revenue per paying workspace, 10,000 workspaces imply RMB 120m ARR; 100,000 imply RMB 1.2bn ARR. These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts; they exclude churn, discounts, cost of service, taxes and sales efficiency.
