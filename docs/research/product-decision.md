# Product and competitive decision — 27 November 2026

## Wedge

The Dream Station is a mobile-first interactive micro-narrative experience built around one original IP candidate. A new user opens a shareable link, begins without registration, completes a 2–4 minute chapter, makes three consequential choices, receives a distinct ending, and can share the result.

NEXORA is an internal project code, not a cleared public brand.

## Competitive findings

- Roblox's Q3 2026 shareholder letter reports 138M average DAUs, 34B hours engaged, $1.6B revenue and $1.7B bookings. Roblox's moat is not AI generation alone: it combines distribution, social graph, creator tooling, discovery, safety and economy. We do not build a low-budget Roblox clone.
- Google Play's 13 October 2026 announcement introduced Gamer DNA and Game Finder, plus curated game playlists. Generic game discovery is therefore not our wedge.
- Episode community discussions repeatedly criticize paywalls on core choices, opaque early-access gates and monetization that interrupts the story. Our main story choices remain free; payment, if later tested, is for optional bonus content or a season pass after demand is proven.
- AI Dungeon community feedback identifies story memory/continuity failures and the model taking control away from the player. Our core narrative is authored, finite-state and reproducible. AI is an internal drafting/translation aid only, not a live dependency.

## Product rules

1. One IP world, 1–3 core characters, three launch chapters.
2. 2–5 meaningful choices per chapter; every choice changes state or outcome.
3. Mainline story choices are never paywalled.
4. No endless feed, open-ended companion, live chat, public UGC, user text submission or fake community metrics in v0.
5. Share links are the acquisition primitive. The receiving user can play their own path without installing an app.
6. Story saves are local and deletable. No account or personal data collection in the first prototype.
7. No payment until an English-market cohort demonstrates completion, repeat use and organic sharing.

## Compliance boundary

The CAC's Interim Measures for Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services took effect 15 July 2026. The prototype avoids persistent simulated-person emotional interaction, free-form user input, open-ended AI replies and dependence-oriented mechanics. Mainland public release requires a separate legal assessment; overseas launch still requires market-specific privacy, consumer, age, IP and payment review.

## Validation gates

- Day 7: IP source/right ledger, 10 user interviews and 5 observed play sessions.
- Day 14: at least 8/20 target users correctly recognize KURU in a mixed-character recognition test; first-session completion target 70%.
- Day 30: 100 qualified adult test users; measure chapter completion, replay, share-link creation and return visits by cohort.
- Day 45: test a second content chapter and friend-link completion.
- Day 60: run a small optional-content willingness-to-pay test only if repeat use and sharing are present.
- Day 90: target D7 ≥15% and D30 ≥8% as internal challenge lines, not industry benchmarks; continue only if contribution margin and organic sharing are credible.

## Stop / pivot rules

If users complete once but do not replay, share or return after two content iterations, stop expanding platform features and revise the IP or interaction mechanism. Do not buy traffic to disguise weak retention.
